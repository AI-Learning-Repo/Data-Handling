# Activity 1: Get to Know Each Other & Form Your Groups

## Why are we doing this?

This course involves a lot of practical work, discussion, and collaboration. Before we start, take some time to get to know the people you will be working with.

You don't need to agree with each other. The goal is to discover **different backgrounds, interests, expectations, and perspectives on AI**.

---

## Step 1 — Introduce Yourself

In your group, briefly introduce yourself:

* What is your name?
* What is your background?
* What made you interested in this course?
* What experience do you already have with AI, programming, or data?

---

## Step 2 — AI & You

Discuss the following questions:

### Would You Want an AI Clone?

If you could create an AI clone of yourself, **would you want one?**

What would you want it to do?

For example:

* Answer messages for you?
* Attend meetings?
* Learn from your knowledge?
* Help you study?
* Represent you online?
* Do something completely different?

---

### If You Had Unlimited Resources...

Imagine that you had **unlimited computing power, data, and money** to build your own Large Language Model.

**What would you build it for?**

Think about:

* A particular industry or profession
* A scientific field
* A specific language or culture
* Education
* Entertainment
* A personal assistant
* Something that does not exist yet

**Why would you choose that purpose?**

---

## Step 3 — The Future of AI

### What Happens If We Reach the Singularity?

The **technological singularity** is a hypothetical point at which technological progress—potentially driven by artificial intelligence—becomes extremely rapid and difficult to predict.

Read the short definition if you are unfamiliar with the concept:

[Technological Singularity — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technological_singularity?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Then discuss:

* What do you think could happen if we reached a technological singularity?
* What could be positive about it?
* What could go wrong?
* **Would you be worried about it? Why or why not?**

There are no correct answers here. The goal is to hear different perspectives.

---

## Step 4 — Your Expectations for This Course

Think about the course ahead.

### What challenges do you think you might face?

For example:

* Programming
* Mathematics
* Understanding machine-learning concepts
* Working with data
* Keeping up with the course
* Working in a group
* Understanding new AI technologies

What would help you overcome those challenges?

Share your current experience with tools and technologies.
